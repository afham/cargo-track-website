## Step-by-Step Checklist for a Seamless Move

To maintain maximum efficiency on moving day, follow this proven timeline recommended by logistics experts:

### Step 1: Pre-Move Audit & Inventory (2 Weeks Before)

Inspect every room to declutter unused items. Donating or discarding unnecessary items reduces your total shipment volume and lowers your overall quote.

### Step 2: Clear Labeling & Room Assignment (Days Before)

Ensure every box is clearly labeled with its destination room and handling priority (e.g., _"Kitchen – Fragile Glassware"_ or _"Master Bedroom"_).

### Step 3: Secure Personal Documents & Valuables

Keep passports, legal documents, jewelry, and personal electronics in a separate bag that you transport personally in your own vehicle.
